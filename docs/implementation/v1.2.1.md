## Contract changes

The editing preview is approximate HTML/CSS and does not gate valid content saves or insertion. Paged.js runs locally in the browser. Exact saved-version PDF review and export validation remain authoritative. Historical checkpoints and exports stay immutable. This supersedes the v1.2 PDF-before-insertion requirement and the older deferred-HTML-preview statement.

Editable drafts using exactly unchanged built-in definitions upgrade automatically. Customized definitions and source are preserved. Library history is immutable; editing/insertion may use compatible updated definitions without rewriting its old revision.
